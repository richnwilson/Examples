Welcome to our new members for 2020!

 
On March 13th 2020 the IBM Academy of Technology has appointed 88 IBMers to join our ranks. Our Academy now has 838 members, including 93 Fellows and 190 Distinguished Engineers.
 
These individuals have not only demonstrated exceptional leadership for IBM’s clients; they have also contributed significantly to our Academy through initiative work and leadership in Affiliates worldwide. An Academy membership is highly sought after as it provides a unique platform to network with the very best and brightest technical minds in IBM (including our Fellows).
 
Please welcome and congratulate our AoT new member class of 2020. They reflect truly the best of IBM.
